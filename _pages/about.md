I am a first-year MPH student in the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics at the [School of Public Health](https://sph.pku.edu.cn), [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn). My supervisor is Researcher [Gao Pei](https://sph.pku.edu.cn/info/1180/3597.htm), and my current research focuses on predictive models for cardiovascular diseases in cancer populations.

<h2 id="education">Educational Background</h2>
I commenced my undergraduate studies at Peking University School of Public Health in September 2020, where I majored in Preventive Medicine. I graduated in June 2025 with a Bachelor of Medicine degree, achieving a GPA of 3.69/4.00 and ranking 6th among 90 students in my major. Concurrently, from September 2021, I pursued a double degree in Psychology at Peking University School of Psychological and Cognitive Sciences, earning a Bachelor of Science upon completion in June 2025. Currently, since September 2025, I have been furthering my education at my alma mater, Peking University School of Public Health, as a Master of Professional Studies student in the Department of Epidemiology and Biostatistics.

<h2 id="research">Research Experience</h2>
From September 2023 to June 2024, I participated in the "Chunhui Research Program" at Peking University School of Public Health. Under the supervision of Professor [Sun Feng](https://sph.pku.edu.cn/info/1182/3600.htm), I engaged in a pharmacoepidemiology study and co-authored the paper titled "Risk factors for tigecycline-associated acute pancreatitis in patients: A multicenter nested case-control study" as a co-first author; the manuscript is currently under review. Subsequently, from December 2024 to July 2025, I contributed to a project within Working Group 4 of the Global Harmonization Working Party for Medical Devices (GHWP). I assisted in developing a framework for transitioning medical device post-market surveillance from passive to active monitoring and helped draft the guideline document "Playbook for Design and Implementation of Active Post-Market Surveillance System," which is presently in the revision stage.

<h2 id="award">Award</h2>
Received the Peking University Academic Excellence Award for the 2020-2021 academic year and the 2023-2024 academic year.

<h2 id="internship">Internship Experience</h2>
From March 2023 to January 2024, I completed a clinical internship at Beijing Haidian Hospital, where I underwent a systematic general medicine rotation. I actively participated in ward management, medical documentation, and outpatient diagnosis and treatment, gaining proficiency in fundamental clinical skills such as physical examination and patient history taking. Subsequently, from June to September 2024, I interned at the Haidian District Center for Disease Control and Prevention, primarily assigned to the Emergency Response Department. During which, I became familiar with disease surveillance and prevention workflows and acquired practical experience in outbreak emergency response and epidemiological investigation reporting.

<h2 id="practical">Practical Experience</h2>
Participated in the Peking University School of Public Health summer ideological and political practice activity in Shanghai and Jiaxing, Zhejiang from June to July 2021. Served as a member of the Sports Department in the School of Public Health Student Union from September 2020 to June 2022, assisting in organizing a series of cultural and sports activities, including fun sports meets and Spring Festival couplet-writing events during winter vacation. Engaged in on-site work for a large-scale randomized controlled trial on cardiovascular diseases in Yinzhou District, Ningbo, Zhejiang from July to September 2025.
